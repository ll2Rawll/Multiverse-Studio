# Launch video

A 1920×1080, 30 fps sequence (`sequences/launch.mvseq.json`) with an opening title card, a lower third and an end card, plus a small page to show the exported video.

1. Open the Video workspace and import footage or a screen recording into Assets.
2. Drag it onto Video 1 between the titles, edit the titles, and export MP4.
3. Optionally add the export to the project as `launch.mp4`.
